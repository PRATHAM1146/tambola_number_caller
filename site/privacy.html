Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who runs this site

Tambola Caller is run by [Operator name]. You can contact us at [contact email].

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Tambola Caller has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts. The game runs entirely in your browser, and the numbers you call are never sent to us.

What is stored on your device

Your voice settings (chosen voice, speed, volume and mute) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are kept the next time you open the page. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Game progress is not saved. Reloading or closing the page ends the current game.

Voice announcements

Numbers are spoken by your browser's built-in speech feature. Many voices run on your device. Some voices, labelled "online" in the voice list (for example the Google voices in Chrome or the "Online (Natural)" voices in Edge), send the text being spoken to the browser maker's servers to produce the audio. The only text sent is the announcement itself, such as "Next number is 42." That processing is covered by Google's or Microsoft's privacy policy. If you prefer that no text leaves your device, choose a voice that is not labelled "online".

Fonts

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as with any web request. See the Google privacy policy for how Google handles this.

Hosting

This site is hosted by [hosting provider]. Like most web hosts, they may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, time of request and the page requested, for security and to keep the service running. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Children

Tambola Caller does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
